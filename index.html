How to play
  • Trap every monster in a bubble, then burst the bubble by touching it. The monster turns into fruit.
  • Pop several trapped bubbles at once for 1000 × 2(n-1) points — 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000…
  • Multi-pops release EXTEND letters. Collect E-X-T-E-N-D for a free life.
  • Empty bubbles are platforms — fall onto one to ride it upward.
  • Monsters that escape a bubble turn angry and move much faster.
  • Take too long and HURRY UP! flashes — then an invincible Skel-Monsta hunts you down.
  • Water, fire and lightning bubbles drift in. Pop one and the element fires away from the way you face.
  • Fall off the bottom of the screen and you reappear at the top.
