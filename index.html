How to play
  • Trap every monster in a bubble, then burst the bubble by touching it. The monster pops into fruit — go pick it up.
  • Burst several trapped bubbles at once for 1000 × 2(n-1) points — 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000…
  • Multi-pops shake loose EXTEND letters. Collect E-X-T-E-N-D for a free life.
  • Empty bubbles are platforms — drop onto one and ride it upward to reach anywhere.
  • Every rung is also within jumping range, so you can always climb on foot.
  • Monsters that escape a bubble turn angry and move much faster.
  • Take too long and HURRY UP! flashes — then an invincible hunter comes for you.
  • Water, fire and lightning bubbles drift in. Pop one and the element fires away from the way you face.
  • Fall off the bottom of the screen and you reappear at the top.
